# Concept C: Lakeside Street

## Original artistic brief

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: landscape environment concept art for a browser-based 3D delivery driving game

Primary request: Create one original, high-quality concept image of a relaxed Colombo lakeside urban street inspired by Beira Lake, Sri Lanka. The scene should help define a believable road environment before 3D implementation.

Scene/backdrop: A modest inland Colombo neighborhood beside the lake. On the inland street edge, show an irregularly spaced mix of distinct 3–5 storey commercial and residential buildings with a few smaller heritage facades. Use varied widths, rooflines, setbacks, balconies, shutters, awnings, weathering, and muted tropical colors; every building should feel individually designed but belong to the same real city. On the water edge, show a practical promenade with mature broadleaf shade trees, occasional palms, low railings, simple benches, and glimpses across calm Beira Lake.

Subject: A coherent two-lane urban road, one lane in each direction, designed for left-hand traffic. Show continuous, clearly visible pedestrian pavement/sidewalk on both road edges, with realistic curbs, driveway cuts, storm drains, gutters, and sensible transitions around trees and entrances. Restrained white center dashes only; absolutely no American yellow road lines. Include only a few ordinary vehicles if helpful, positioned consistently with left-hand traffic, and parking bays only where there is believable room.

Style/medium: High-quality stylized realism for a modern 3D game environment concept, grounded and more detailed than low-poly, with physically plausible scale and materials. It should look like polished environment concept art or a realistic game-engine target render, not a documentary photograph.

Composition/framing: One medium-wide 16:9 landscape frame from a low approximately 3-metre elevated street camera, looking diagonally along the road so asphalt texture, curbs, both sidewalks, building facades, mature trees, promenade, and lake are all legible. The road is the main spatial anchor. Place Colombo's Lotus Tower far in the distance as a small but recognizable landmark with its pink lotus crown, correctly proportioned and integrated into the skyline; it must not loom over the road or dominate the composition.

Lighting/mood: Warm soft golden-hour sunlight balanced by cooler reflected lake ambience, relaxed everyday urban atmosphere, believable shadows and atmospheric depth.

Color palette: Natural, restrained Colombo tropical colors—weathered concrete, muted plaster, dusty greens, warm late-day highlights, cool blue-green water.

Materials/textures: Matte weathered asphalt with subtle patch repairs and repair seams; concrete curbs and pavements; realistic drainage details; aged painted plaster, masonry, timber and metal facade details; natural variation in foliage, bark, and ground wear.

Constraints: Preserve recognizable urban Colombo character and plausible human scale. The lakefront is civic and everyday, not a luxury resort. Lotus Tower remains small, distant, recognizable, and correctly proportioned. Buildings must have natural variety and irregular spacing. No required readable text.

Avoid: identical copy-paste buildings; repetitive procedural facades; fantasy or futuristic architecture; oversized Lotus Tower; tower immediately beside the street; American yellow lines; right-hand traffic; broken or missing sidewalks; pristine plastic surfaces; excessive traffic; excessive parked cars; tropical resort styling; overgrown jungle; implausibly lush vegetation; dramatic tourist-postcard spectacle; photoreal photographic treatment; low-poly look; HUD; UI; captions; titles; watermarks; readable logos or branding; hero characters.

## Final image-edit instruction

Use case: precise-object-edit

Asset type: landscape environment concept art for a browser-based 3D delivery driving game

Primary request: Remove every motor vehicle from the image, including all cars and the tuk-tuk, at every distance. Reconstruct the newly exposed road surface naturally with the same matte weathered asphalt, subtle repair seams, white lane markings, perspective, shadows, and golden-hour illumination already present.

Constraints: This is a tightly targeted removal edit. Keep the original canvas size, crop, camera angle, composition, architecture, storefronts, sidewalks, curbs, drains, road geometry and markings, pedestrians, benches, railings, streetlights, planters, trees, foliage, lake, reflections, sky, distant skyline, Lotus Tower, color palette, material detail, atmosphere, and lighting unchanged. Preserve all non-vehicle content and its exact placement as closely as possible. Do not remove or alter any people.

Avoid: adding replacement vehicles, bicycles, motorcycles, tuk-tuks, buses, carts, new people, new objects, text, logos, or changing the cityscape, lakefront, skyline, foliage, weather, time of day, or overall style.
