# Concept B: Neighborhood Shops

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: environment concept art for a browser-based 3D Colombo delivery game
Primary request: Create one imaginative, realistic street perspective of a lively Colombo neighborhood commercial street with an authentic tropical low-rise townscape. It should feel specific, lived-in, inviting, and feasible to build as a 3D game environment.
Scene/backdrop: A believable two-lane asphalt road with one lane in each direction, arranged for Sri Lankan left-hand traffic. Use a restrained broken white centerline. The road is cared for but weathered, with visible asphalt aggregate, subtle repairs, and a few natural patches. Concrete curbs border continuous pedestrian pavements with small believable level changes and driveway transitions. Include a sensible pedestrian crossing only at a distant junction, not in the foreground. On both sides are varied two-to-four-storey independent shop-houses: deep awnings, occasional verandah columns, modest balconies, some roller doors, weathered cream, ochre, and muted teal plaster, distinct rooflines, and occasional gaps revealing small courtyards. Broadleaf tropical street trees are irregularly spaced but organized, with a few planters and simple street lamps. Add credible Colombo utility wires, poles, gutters, and drainage details without visual chaos.
Subject: The street environment itself; sparse pedestrians and one parked tuk-tuk may provide scale. No central rider or hero character.
Style/medium: High-quality stylized-realism 3D game environment concept, physically credible proportions, construction, surfaces, and materials; detailed enough to guide later 3D building. Avoid a plastic blockout look and avoid hyperreal photographic fantasy.
Composition/framing: Landscape image, one coherent street scene from approximately 2 metres camera height, slightly oblique cinematic street perspective that clearly shows shop frontage, pavement, curb, and the road continuing toward the distant junction. Natural depth and a buildable streetscape layout.
Lighting/mood: Bright soft tropical daylight after rain; mostly dry, matte pavement with only restrained residual dampness; warm, active neighborhood mood without dramatic spectacle.
Color palette: Weathered cream, ochre, muted teal, charcoal asphalt, concrete grey, rich tropical green; natural and restrained.
Materials/textures: Fine asphalt aggregate and patching, worn plaster, concrete curbs and drains, painted metal shutters, timber and metal awnings, believable foliage.
Text: No readable text.
Constraints: Left-hand traffic logic must be spatially believable. One lane each direction. Broken restrained white centerline. Crossing only at the distant junction. Continuous usable pavements. Varied independent facades and rooflines. No cloned row of identical buildings. Moderate detail with clear spatial hierarchy.
Avoid: excessive clutter, traffic jam, crowds, foreground zebra crossing, pristine resort streets, giant towers, colonial monument centerpiece, cloned facades, exaggerated neon, readable brand names, logos, captions, HUD, watermark, rider hero, wet mirror-like road, fisheye lens, aerial view.
