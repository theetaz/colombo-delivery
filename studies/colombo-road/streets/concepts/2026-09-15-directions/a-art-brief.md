# Concept A: Green Neighborhood

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: visual development concept for a polished browser-based 3D delivery game

Primary request: Create one original imagined green neighborhood street in contemporary Colombo, Sri Lanka. It should feel believable, inviting, lived-in, and suitable as a target for a real-time game environment, without reconstructing any exact existing street.

Scene/backdrop: A two-lane neighborhood road, one lane in each direction, configured for Sri Lankan left-hand traffic. Matte asphalt with subtle patch repairs and restrained short white center dashes. Continuous genuinely walkable pavements on both sides, practical curb ramps, open and covered roadside drainage appropriate to Colombo, and credible driveway crossings. Mature tropical broadleaf shade trees form an irregular green canopy while leaving architecture visible. Both sides contain varied two- and three-storey homes and a few modest corner shops, with plaster walls, weathered boundary walls, metal gates, shaded verandas, balconies, tiled or shallow-pitched roofs, small setbacks, gaps and lush gardens between buildings. Add subtle utility poles, overhead cables, meter boxes, drains, streetlights, parked scooters or bicycles only where natural.

Style/medium: High-quality stylized realism CGI for a polished contemporary game. Physically plausible scale, architecture, road engineering, vegetation, materials, reflections, weathering, and ambient occlusion. Rich natural texture and detail; grounded South Asian tropical urban character. Avoid a plastic low-poly aesthetic and avoid photofantasy or exaggerated cinematic spectacle.

Composition/framing: Single wide landscape image. Camera at street level, approximately 2 metres high, near the edge of a foreground pavement. Strong, readable road vanishing point leading into the neighborhood. Include tactile foreground pavement, curb, drain, roots or leaf litter details, with architecture framing both sides and enough mid-distance depth to judge the world design. Natural human eye perspective, around a 28–35mm full-frame equivalent, no extreme wide-angle distortion.

Lighting/mood: Warm late-afternoon sun filtered through humid tropical foliage, soft long shadows, believable sky and atmospheric depth, calm and welcoming everyday mood.

Color palette: Natural warm plaster, muted gates and walls, charcoal asphalt, restrained road paint, deep layered greens, small lived-in color accents.

Traffic: Keep the road mostly quiet. If any moving vehicles appear, they must obey left-hand traffic: vehicles moving away use the left lane and approaching vehicles use the right lane. Prefer sparse, unambiguous traffic.

Constraints: This is original imagined architecture, not an exact street reconstruction. Clear continuous pavement circulation and realistic drainage must be visible. No central yellow line. No game HUD, delivery rider hero, prominent people, logos, readable branding, captions, labels, signs with readable text, watermark, fantasy skyline, landmark monument, neon cyberpunk treatment, spotless suburban uniformity, dense traffic, or implausibly huge trees/buildings.
